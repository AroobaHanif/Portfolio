# Identity kit (for the Claude Project)

- Heading font: Space Grotesk (600-700). Body font: IBM Plex Sans (400).
- Palette: Ink #1A1D23 (text), Paper #F7F6F2 (background), Signal #24304A (main), Amber #C98A2B (single accent, used sparingly).
- Mark: monogram "A" in a Signal rounded square with a small Amber node at the apex (favicon.svg).
- Style note: A calm, technical hand. Space Grotesk carries the ideas, plain-set Plex Sans carries the reasoning. One deep signal blue holds the page together; amber shows up only where something actually needs a second look.
